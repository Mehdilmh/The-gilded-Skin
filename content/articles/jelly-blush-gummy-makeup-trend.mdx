Pinterest's annual trend report named **"Gimme Gummy"** — squishy, bouncy, glossy textures like jelly blushes and elastic cheek tints — as one of 2026's defining beauty trends, according to [Cosmetics Business](https://cosmeticsbusiness.com/pinterest-2026-beauty-trend-predictions-glitchy-glam-and-cool-blue). Jelly blush is the easiest way to try it.

## What jelly blush is

Jelly blushes are water- or gel-based cheek tints with a wobbly, jelly-like texture. Instead of sitting on top of the skin like powder, they stain it, leaving a sheer, glassy flush that tends to last.

## Safety basics

Use products only where their labels say they're suitable — a cheek tint isn't automatically safe for lips or eyes. The FDA's [cosmetic safety tips](https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/resources-consumers-cosmetics/using-cosmetics-safely) also recommend not sharing makeup and keeping your tools clean.

## Common jelly blush mistakes

- **Applying too much at once.** Stains build quickly and are hard to soften once set. Start with a dot.
- **Blending slowly.** Work fast with fingertips before it grabs.
- **Applying over thick powder.** Gel tints can patch over heavy powder; use them before powder or on bare skin.
- **Using it on lips or eyes without checking the label.** Not every cheek tint is suitable for other areas.

## The bottom line

Jelly blush is an easy, low-commitment way into one of 2026's biggest trends: a tiny amount, quick blending and thin layers give a fresh, glassy flush that lasts. Keep the rest of your look sheer and glossy to match.
