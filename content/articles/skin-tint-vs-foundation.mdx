Lighter, "your skin but better" bases — skin tints, tinted sunscreens and serum foundations — are one of the defining makeup trends of 2026. They're not for everyone or every day, though. Here's how to choose.

## Choosing by skin type

- **Dry skin:** creamy or liquid formats over a good moisturizer are usually most comfortable; powder can cling to dry patches.
- **Oily or combination skin:** powder and matte liquids help control shine.
- **Sensitive skin:** fewer layers and fragrance-free formulas are a good starting point.

## About tinted SPF

A tinted SPF is a convenient way to combine steps, but it only protects you if you apply enough — the same generous amount you'd use of a regular sunscreen. The AAD recommends [broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher](https://www.aad.org/media/stats/prevention-and-care/sunscreen-faqs). If you prefer a thin layer of tint, wear a dedicated sunscreen underneath. ## The bottom line

Skin tints and tinted SPFs are perfect for easy, natural days; foundations give you control when you want more coverage. Start with your skin type and the finish you like, make sure your shade exists, and keep sunscreen as its own step unless you're applying a generous layer.
