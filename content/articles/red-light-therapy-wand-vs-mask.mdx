Red light therapy has moved from dermatology offices to bathroom shelves, and Google searches for it reached an all-time high in 2025. The two most common at-home formats are handheld wands and full-face masks. They use similar light, but they ask very different things of you.

## How the two formats differ

A **wand** is a small handheld device with a panel of LEDs that you hold against one area of the face at a time. A **mask** covers the whole face at once and is usually worn hands-free for a timed session.

## What the evidence says

The research on red light for skin is real but limited. A dermatologist interviewed by [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/select/shopping/red-light-therapy-rcna253588) noted that there aren't yet enough human studies to make firm claims about reducing wrinkles or treating photoaging, and that many studies are funded by companies that make red light products. A 2018 [systematic review of randomized trials](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29356026/) found its strongest support for LED use in acne and some wound-healing uses, with many small studies and widely varying treatment settings.

The practical takeaway: expect modest, gradual changes at best, and treat dramatic before-and-after claims with caution. Our guide to [LED face masks](/led-face-masks-what-to-compare) goes deeper into the research.

Many listings say "FDA cleared." Under FDA's [510(k) process](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/premarket-submissions-selecting-and-preparing-correct-submission/premarket-notification-510k), clearance means the device was found substantially equivalent to one already on the market. It's a useful signal, but it doesn't confirm every advertising claim. Search the number in FDA's [510(k) database](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpmn/pmn.cfm) before you buy.

## The bottom line

The shape of the device matters less than its specifications and whether you'll use it consistently. Choose a wand for targeted, portable use and a mask for hands-free coverage, verify any FDA claim yourself, and keep expectations realistic.
