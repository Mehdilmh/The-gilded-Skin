## Ingredients to look for — and avoid

For dry or chapped lips, the American Academy of Dermatology's [lip-care tips](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/skin-care-basics/dry/heal-dry-chapped-lips) suggest ingredients such as castor seed oil, ceramides, dimethicone, mineral oil, petrolatum and shea butter. They also list ingredients that can irritate dry lips, including fragrance, flavorings such as cinnamon, citrus and mint, camphor, eucalyptus and menthol. If a product burns, stings or tingles, the AAD advises stopping it.

## Common glossy-lip mistakes

- **Applying gloss over flaky lips.** Shine highlights texture. Smooth lips with an overnight balm first.
- **Choosing tingling "plumping" formulas for dry lips.** Ingredients that tingle can irritate chapped lips.
- **Forgetting sun protection.** Most glosses and oils don't contain SPF.
- **Sharing lip products.** It's an easy way to pass on cold sores and other infections.

## The bottom line

Pick gloss for shine and color, oil for comfort and a lighter feel. Whichever you choose, skip irritating ingredients if your lips are dry, protect them from the sun, and give them a thick balm overnight.
