## Eye makeup safety

The FDA's [guidance on using cosmetics safely](https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/resources-consumers-cosmetics/using-cosmetics-safely) has specific rules for the eye area:

- Use only products labeled as safe for the eyes — not lip or cheek products.
- Don't share eye makeup.
- Never add water or saliva to products like mascara.
- Throw away eye makeup you used during an eye infection.

Clean brushes matter too — see [how to clean makeup brushes](/how-to-clean-makeup-brushes).

## Common mistakes with blue eyeshadow

- **Going bold everywhere at once.** A saturated blue lid plus heavy liner, contour and a dark lip can look costume-like. Let the eyes be the one statement.
- **Skipping blending.** Hard edges are what make blue look dated. Diffuse the outer edge with a clean, fluffy brush.
- **Choosing the wrong blue for the occasion.** Icy pastels and soft navy are easiest for daytime; electric and metallic blues work best at night.
- **Forgetting the brows.** Groomed, natural brows frame a colorful eye and keep the look polished.
- **Using old products.** Eye makeup has a shorter shelf life than face products. Replace mascara every few months and discard anything that smells or looks different.

## The bottom line

Cool Blue is easier to wear than it looks: start with a sheer wash or smudged liner, keep the rest of your face soft, and build up when you're ready. Use eye-safe products only, and keep your tools clean.
