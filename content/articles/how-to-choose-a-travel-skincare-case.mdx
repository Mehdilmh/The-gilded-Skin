A travel case should make your routine easier away from home, not just hold it. The best choice depends on how you travel — carry-on only or checked bags, hotels or hostels, weekend trips or long stays — so this guide focuses on the features that matter, rather than on a single "best" bag.

## Start with the rule that shapes everything

If you fly with carry-on luggage in the U.S., TSA's [liquids rule](https://www.tsa.gov/travel/security-screening/liquids-rule) determines how much skincare you can bring through security. Liquids, gels and aerosols must be in travel-sized containers of **3.4 ounces (100 milliliters) or less**, and TSA limits each passenger to [one quart-sized bag](https://www.tsa.gov/travel/frequently-asked-questions/what-3-1-1-liquids-rule). Larger containers belong in checked baggage.

The container size is what counts, not how much is left inside. A full-size moisturizer that's nearly empty still exceeds the limit.

<Callout title="Check before you fly" tone="tip">
  Screening procedures and exceptions can change, and other countries apply their own rules. Check TSA's website — or the rules for your departure airport — before each trip.
</Callout>

## Packing to prevent leaks

- **Pack only your daily routine.** Travel is a good time to use the [simple morning routine](/how-to-build-a-simple-morning-skincare-routine) approach: cleanser, moisturizer, sunscreen and any essential treatment.
- **Decant into labeled travel containers** and keep the originals' instructions in mind.
- **Close caps fully** and keep liquids together in their own sealed bag inside the case.
- **Separate liquids from tools and electronics.**
- **Clean the case after each trip** so residue doesn't build up.

## The bottom line

Size the case to your real routine, prioritize upright storage and a wipeable lining, and keep carry-on liquids in a separate quart-size bag that's easy to remove. At home, the same organizing principles apply — see our guide to [organizing skincare on a vanity](/how-to-organize-a-skincare-routine).
