These five fragrances turn up again and again in women's fragrance best-seller lists, and they're easy to confuse. Three are part of the same Marc Jacobs Daisy line, and two more promise a "fresh" floral feel. This guide lines them up by what the brands and major retailers publish: the notes, the concentration and the overall style.

## The Daisy family: which fruit leads

All three Daisy fragrances are fruity florals with a soft, approachable style. The easiest way to choose between them is by which fruit you'd prefer to notice first.

**Daisy**, the original, is listed by Marc Jacobs with violet leaves and wild strawberries on top, violet petals and jasmine in the heart, and musk and vanilla in the base. (One of the brand's own listings gives sandalwood as the base instead, a reminder that note lists vary.) It's the gentlest of the three on paper.

**Daisy Dream** swaps strawberry for blackberry, grapefruit and pear, and adds lychee and blue wisteria to the jasmine heart. Coconut water in the base points to a lighter, more watery feel.

**Daisy Ever So Fresh** is the juiciest: mandarin, mango and pineapple, then rosewater and orange blossom over cashmere woods. It's also the only one of the three sold as an eau de parfum, though, as our [perfume buying guide](/how-to-choose-a-perfume-online) explains, that label isn't standardized.

## Perfect: florals without the fruit

Marc Jacobs **Perfect** eau de toilette takes a different direction. Sephora lists pink peppercorn, polygonum and cedarwood as key notes, and Ulta adds white daffodil as the heart. With no fruit in the published notes, it's the one to look at if the Daisy line sounds too sweet.

One caution: Perfect comes as an eau de toilette, an eau de parfum and Perfect Intense, in similar bottles. Check the listing title for the version you want.

## Acqua di Gioia: the freshest of the five

Armani's **Acqua di Gioia** eau de parfum is the outlier in style. Armani's own pages list primofiore lemon and mint on top, jasmine in the heart, and cedar with a brown-sugar accord in the base, which it describes as amber-woody. Citrus and mint make it the most "fresh" by its notes, with the brown sugar adding a little warmth underneath.

Don't confuse it with Acqua di Giò, Armani's separate men's line with a similar name.

## The bottom line

On their published notes, the Daisy trio covers soft fruity florals from gentle (Daisy) to airy (Daisy Dream) to juicy (Ever So Fresh). Perfect is the pick if you want florals without fruit, and Acqua di Gioia if you want citrus and freshness. If you'd rather start with something lighter and cheaper than a perfume, our [body mist vs. perfume guide](/body-mist-vs-perfume) compares the options, and [scent stacking](/scent-stacking-fragrance-layering) explains how to layer what you already own.
