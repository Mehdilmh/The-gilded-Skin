Automatic curling irons promise salon-style curls without the wrist-twisting technique of a traditional iron. They can genuinely make curling easier — with a few caveats.

## How automatic curlers work

There are two main designs. In one, you place a section of hair against the opening and the device draws it into a heated chamber. In the other, the barrel itself rotates and winds the hair around it. Either way, the device does the wrapping, and many beep when the curl is done.

## Heat damage: what matters most

Research published in the [Journal of Cosmetic Science](https://library.scconline.org/v062n02/55) found that the onset of thermal damage to hair is strongly temperature dependent. Dermatologists quoted by [WebMD](https://www.webmd.com/beauty/features/how-not-to-wreck-your-hair?page=2) also recommend avoiding heat on wet hair and using protective styling products.

## The bottom line

An automatic curling iron is a smart pick if consistency and ease matter more to you than flexibility. Choose one with safety features, keep sections small, use the lowest temperature that works and always protect your hair from heat.
