## Common beauty-gifting mistakes

- **Guessing a skincare routine.** Serums and treatments depend on skin type and sensitivities; tools and accessories don't.
- **Guessing a shade.** For foundation or concealer, only buy if you know the exact shade — or choose sheer, forgiving products like gloss.
- **Ignoring hair type.** Hot tools suit some hair lengths and textures better than others. Check the product's guidance.
- **Buying a device they haven't asked for.** Devices need time and consistency. Choose one only for someone who's already curious.
- **Forgetting the gift receipt.** It makes any miss easy to fix.

## The bottom line

Give to their habits: patches for the self-care lover, a sheer gloss for the lip fan, a verified device for the gadget fan, the right hot tool for the stylist and a rotary kit for the groomer. Check shades and hair types, and always include a gift receipt. For the fragrance lover, a few perfume oils make a lovely set for [scent stacking](/scent-stacking-fragrance-layering).
